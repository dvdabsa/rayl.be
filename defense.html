/ 01 — Doctrine

The decisive edge is no longer only steel. It is software.

The speed at which raw data becomes a decision determines outcomes. Rayl Defense exists to compress that loop — bringing product-company speed, modern AI and honest engineering to the institutions that protect open societies.

Software only. Rayl Technologies BV develops software and analysis tooling. We do not design, produce or trade weapons or weapon components, and we assess every engagement against EU dual-use and export-control rules and applicable sanctions regimes.
