About Rayl (boilerplate)

Rayl Technologies BV is a Belgian software and technology company headquartered in Antwerp. Rayl develops, operates, and commercializes digital platforms, software solutions, online marketplaces, and related technology services — including SaaS, digital infrastructure, automation tools, e-commerce technology, and fintech platforms — for businesses and consumers. Revenue is generated through subscriptions, licensing, platform fees, transaction-based fees, software sales, and technology services. Today, Rayl’s flagship products are Rayl Payments, a simpler way to send and receive money, and Rayl Marketplace, a seamless place to sell. Rayl’s mission is to open up fintech and digital commerce to everyone, everywhere.
