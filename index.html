How we work

A four-step process, no surprises.

Same shape every engagement. We scope honestly, design in the open, ship in milestones, and stay on after launch.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We dig into the problem with you. Goals, constraints, users, success metrics. Output: a concrete scope & quote — usually within a week.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architecture, data model, UX, integrations. We share progress as we go — no month-long silence. You see decisions before they ship.

  3. 03

    Build

    Senior engineers writing production code from day one. Milestones every 1–2 weeks. Demos every Friday. Code on your GitHub, your stack, your ownership.

  4. 04

    Operate

    We don’t disappear at launch. Support, monitoring, iterations — switch to a retainer or hand it off cleanly. Your call.
