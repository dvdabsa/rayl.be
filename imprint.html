Activities

Rayl Technologies BV is a software and technology business. We develop, operate, and commercialize digital platforms, software solutions, online marketplaces, and related technology services — including SaaS, digital infrastructure, automation tools, e-commerce technology, and fintech platforms. Revenue is generated through subscriptions, licensing, platform fees, transaction-based fees, software sales, and technology services.

Regulated activities & supervision

Rayl Technologies BV is not a licensed payment institution and does not itself hold or move customer funds. Payment services accessed through Rayl Payments are provided by licensed third-party Payment Service Providers (PSPs) authorised under PSD2 and supervised by their respective EU national competent authorities (in Belgium, the National Bank of Belgium – NBB). The identity of the PSP(s) operating in Rayl Payments is disclosed in product and available on request from hello@rayl.be.
