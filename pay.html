04 — Integrate

Live in
minutes.

Drop a script tag, mount a button, take payments. That's the entire flow.

checkout.html 
<!-- 1. Drop in Rayl Pay -->
<script src="https://js.raylpay.io/v1"></script>

<!-- 2. Mount a checkout button -->
<div id="pay"></div>

<script>
  const rayl = new RaylPay({
    publicKey: "pk_live_...",
    methods: ["card", "apple_pay", "google_pay"],
    amount: 4900,        // in cents
    currency: "EUR",
    onSuccess: (tx) => console.log("paid", tx.id),
  });

  rayl.mount("#pay");
</script>

07 — Security

Designed to protect.

/ 01

3D Secure 2

Frictionless step-up authentication built in. Liability shift handled automatically where supported.

/ 02

Tokenization

Card data never touches your servers. We exchange it for a token you can store, charge, and refund safely.

/ 03

Real-time fraud scoring

Every transaction is scored against device, network, and behavioral signals before it reaches the issuer.

/ 04

PCI scope reduction

Our hosted fields and drop-in element keep you out of the part of PCI compliance you don't want to be in.

/ 05

Chargeback workflows

Disputes surface in the dashboard with evidence templates, deadlines, and a one-click submit flow.

/ 06

Encrypted end-to-end

TLS in transit. AES-256 at rest. Keys rotated on a schedule, not on a hope.
