Anatomy of a payment

From tap to authorized,
in under 200 ms.

Every Rayl Pay charge runs the same path — risk-scored, routed, and authorized at the edge before it ever reaches your server.

  1. 01

    Checkout

    Hosted page, drop-in element, or native SDK collects card or wallet data on the client.

  3. 02

    Risk engine

    200+ device, geo, behavioral, and BIN signals are scored against our model in milliseconds.

  5. 03

    Network

    Routed to the right rail — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay or Google Pay — with regional failover.

  7. 04

    Authorized

    Webhook fires, signed and idempotent. Your dashboard updates in real time.

create_charge.ts 
// 1. Create a charge
import { Rayl } from "@raylpay/node";

const rayl = new Rayl(process.env.RAYL_SECRET_KEY);

const charge = await rayl.charges.create({
  amount: 4900,             // €49.00
  currency: "EUR",
  methods: ["card", "apple_pay", "google_pay"],
  customer: "cus_2Nq…",
  metadata: { order_id: "4821" },
});

// 2. Webhook arrives signed and idempotent

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Rayl Pay is a single integration that lets you accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay across web, mobile, and in-person checkout — with one API, one dashboard, and one reconciliation flow.
Most teams take their first test payment inside an hour with the hosted checkout. Custom integrations using the drop-in element or REST API typically ship within a few days.
Card data is tokenized in the browser and never reaches your servers. Combined with hosted fields and end-to-end encryption, you stay out of the most expensive parts of PCI compliance.
