Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

1. Who we are

Rayl BV ("Rayl", "we", "us") is a Belgian company with registered seat in Belgium and enterprise number BE 0717.928.771. We can be reached at hello@rayl.be for any questions about this policy.

2. What this policy covers

This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you visit our website (rayl.be), use any of our products (including Rayl Pay), or contact us. It is intended to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Belgian data-protection law.

3. Data we collect

We collect only the data we need to operate our products and respond to you:

  • Contact data you give us — name, email address, company, the message itself — when you reach out to us, request access, or apply for a role.
  • Technical data automatically logged when you visit the site — IP address, browser type, device, and pages viewed.
  • Product data if you become a Rayl Pay user — account details, integration metadata, and transaction-level information processed under a separate Data Processing Agreement.

4. How we use it

  • To answer your messages and provide the services you ask for (legal basis: legitimate interest / performance of contract).
  • To keep the site and our products secure and operational (legitimate interest).
  • To comply with our legal obligations, including financial, fiscal, and anti-money-laundering law.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as we need to for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law. Contact form submissions are kept for up to 24 months. Product data is governed by the relevant product agreement.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, and to object to processing or request portability. To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@rayl.be.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données) at dataprotectionauthority.be.

7. Cookies

This site uses a minimal set of strictly necessary cookies. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we'll ask for your consent first.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of the page and — for material changes — notify you directly where appropriate.

This policy is provided in good faith and represents Rayl's current practices. It is not a substitute for legal advice. If you have a specific question, contact us.
